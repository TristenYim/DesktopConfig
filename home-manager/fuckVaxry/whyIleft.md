# Why I'm Leaving Hyprland

## Some Context

This is a quick message to anyone who sees this, I suggest looking into the topics I discuss further before drawing conclusions.

I started out my Linux journey on Hyprland, based on the recommendation of a close friend.
Hyprland grew to become important to me due its strong customizability and large featureset compared to anything I had on Windows.

However, problems became apparent as I used it overtime.
In part due to it being so bleeding edge, I constantly ran into bugs that ruined my day on Hyprland.

In addition, Vaxry is what I would call a highly opinionated developer. He unashamedly does things his own way, both for the better and the worse.
As a small example of this, see [https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland/issues/2930]. Sure, this problem is very easy to solve by changing one line in the 
config, but why should the default behavior in the first place be to show an anime girl flashbang the screen half the time? It just comes off as
unprofessional, and this only becomes more apparent the more you observe Vaxry's behavior online.

## The Big Problem

At some point (long after I started using Hyprland), I became aware of a controversy surrounding Hyprland and Vaxry.
I suggest any and all of you read the context surrounding it here [https://drewdevault.com/2023/09/17/Hyprland-toxicity.html],
but TLDR, Vaxry supports transphobia and tries to justify himself with sloppy excuses.

His behavior as a developer was already bad enough, but I draw the line here with his behavior towards his community.
Considering how popular of a compositor Hyprland is on places such as r/unixporn, I am certain that Vaxry's beliefs
are influencing younger generations, and ultimately causing harm to trans people (and everyone).

I'm keeping this here to send a message: Please, DO NOT use Hyprland.
